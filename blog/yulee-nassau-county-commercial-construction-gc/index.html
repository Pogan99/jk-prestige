Commercial Construction in Yulee and Nassau County: A GC's Ground-Level Guide

June 8, 2026 · 6 min read · JK Prestige Constructor Corp

Yulee and the broader Nassau County corridor — from the Nassau-Duval County line north to Fernandina Beach and west toward Hilliard — is one of the most active commercial construction zones in Northeast Florida right now. Population growth in Nassau County has been running 5 to 8 percent annually, driven by residents moving out of Duval County and by logistics and light industrial users drawn to lower land costs and proximity to I-95 and the Kings Bay corridor.

If you are planning a commercial project in Yulee — retail, office, medical, or industrial — the construction environment is different from Jacksonville in ways that affect your budget, schedule, and contractor selection.

Nassau County Permitting vs. Duval County

Nassau County building permits are processed through the Nassau County Building Department for unincorporated areas and through the City of Fernandina Beach for projects within that city's limits. The City of Yulee does not have an independent building department — Yulee is an unincorporated community in Nassau County, so permits run through the county.

Plan review turnaround: Nassau County commercial plan review runs 4 to 8 weeks for straightforward commercial projects. More complex projects (multi-use, MEP-heavy, healthcare) can run 8 to 12 weeks. This is comparable to Duval County but not faster — a common misconception.

State agency coordination: Projects adjacent to US-17, A1A, or SR-200 may require Florida DOT driveway permits or access management review. Nassau County's SR-200 (Yulee Road) corridor has active DOT access management controls. JK includes DOT coordination in the pre-construction scope on projects near these corridors.

Flood zone and FEMA: Significant portions of Nassau County are in FEMA Zone AE or Zone X (shaded) based on proximity to the Nassau River, Amelia River, and associated wetland systems. Confirm the flood zone for any Nassau County site before finalizing site design.

Utility Availability in Yulee

This is the single biggest variable that drives commercial development cost in Yulee. Municipal water and sewer service in Nassau County is provided by Nassau County Utility Services for most of unincorporated Nassau, including Yulee. Service extension is available along established corridors but is not universal.

New service connections: If your site requires a new water or sewer connection, or if the existing utility main is not adjacent to the site, the cost of extending service can be significant. JK requests utility availability confirmation from Nassau County Utility Services during pre-construction before writing any estimate that includes utility connection.

Septic permits: For sites outside the utility service area, an onsite septic system with a Florida DOH permit through the Nassau County Health Department may be the path. Septic systems for commercial uses have specific requirements for daily flow, setbacks, and system type.

Three-phase electrical: Three-phase power availability in Yulee is growing but not ubiquitous. Duke Energy and Florida Power & Light serve different areas of Nassau County. For commercial projects requiring significant three-phase electrical service, JK confirms transformer availability and extension costs early in pre-construction. This is a 4 to 12 week utility lead item and needs to be on the schedule from day one.

What Commercial Projects Are Active in Yulee

The most common commercial project types in the Yulee corridor:

Retail and restaurant. Strip center development and QSR (quick service restaurant) pad sites are active along SR-200 and US-17. Drive-through configurations are common and require turn lane and stacking calculations reviewed by Nassau County.

Medical and dental. The population growth in Nassau County is driving demand for primary care, dental, and urgent care facilities. JK has delivered medical office fit-outs and has experience with the AHCA licensing process for medical facilities in Florida.

Light industrial and warehouse. Nassau County offers industrial land at $2 to $4 per square foot in some areas — well below comparable sites in Duval County. The Yulee Industrial Park and properties along US-1 north of Yulee Road are seeing consistent activity.

Self-storage. Nassau County's residential growth has driven demand for self-storage. Multi-story climate-controlled self-storage is a frequent project type. Permitting for self-storage is straightforward; the site plan (access, setbacks from residential zones) typically drives the most review time.

Working in Nassau County as a Jacksonville GC

A GC licensed in Florida can operate anywhere in the state, but local knowledge matters in Nassau County. Specific items JK accounts for:

Subcontractor availability. Some specialty subs who primarily serve Duval County add a mobilization premium for Nassau County projects. JK maintains relationships with Nassau County-based electricians, plumbers, and concrete subs who are already working locally.

Material staging. Yulee is 25 to 35 minutes from most Jacksonville supply yards. On large projects, material deliveries are scheduled in windows to avoid repeated trips.

Inspection scheduling. Nassau County inspection staff serve all of unincorporated Nassau County — a large geographic area. Inspection windows can run 48 to 72 hours from request. JK builds this into the construction schedule so inspections do not sit on the critical path.

How long does commercial permitting take in Nassau County?
4 to 8 weeks for straightforward commercial projects. Projects with significant MEP complexity, DOT coordination, or state agency review can run 8 to 12 weeks. JK submits permit applications at the earliest possible stage of design to minimize impact on the project schedule.

What is the cost of commercial construction in Yulee per square foot?
Commercial construction in Yulee generally runs $80 to $150 per square foot for office, retail, and medical, depending on the fit-out level and MEP complexity. Industrial shell construction runs $55 to $75 per square foot. These are building costs; site work and utility extensions are additional.
Are there any construction restrictions near Amelia Island?
Properties on or adjacent to Amelia Island (which falls within the City of Fernandina Beach or unincorporated Nassau County) may be subject to local historic preservation review, tree protection ordinances, and coastal setback requirements. JK reviews all applicable overlay regulations during pre-construction.
Does Nassau County require a specific contractor license for commercial work?
Florida law requires a licensed general contractor (CGC license) or certified building contractor for commercial construction. JK holds the appropriate Florida licenses.
